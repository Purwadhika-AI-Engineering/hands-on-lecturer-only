# Acme Store Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund within 14 days of delivery for unused items in their original packaging.
Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 business days after we receive the item.

Food and coffee products can only be refunded if they arrive damaged or expired. Please send a photo of the damaged item to support.

Shipping costs are not refundable unless the item arrived damaged or was not what you ordered.
